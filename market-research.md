2. European Market
European audiobook market: $2.08B in 2025, up from $850M in 2020 — more than doubled in 5 years at CAGR of 18.61%.

Projected to reach $20.87B by 2033 at CAGR 26.54%. 
2026 projection: ~$2.5B

Key facts:

Germany = 32% of European market. UK is the largest single country market 
Fiction = 56.4% of European market share 
Only 12% of audiobooks released in 2023 were in regional European languages.
40%+ of Europeans consumed audio-based content during commute or leisure in 2023 

Market leaders in Europe: Audible, Storytel, Bookbeat, Podimo.
Us vs European market: Storytel is the closest competitor — 2.67M subscribers, ~$340M revenue — but has zero AI features, social context or note taking ability.
