## Ukrainian Audiobook Market

### Current State

- Only **0.5% of Ukrainian book consumers** purchased audiobooks as of 2023 — but the market is growing rapidly
- Ukrainian book market total: ~6B UAH (~€140–150M) — less than 1% of European market despite Ukraine being 8%+ of EU population by size
- Main local players: **Абук**, **MEGOGO AUDIO**, Студія ВУХО, Underbooks

### Абук — Market Leader Data

- **120,000 audiobooks sold** in 2025 (plus 30,000 e-books)
- **15 million UAH** paid to rights holders as royalties in 2025
- Catalog grew by 180+ new titles in 2025
- **20% of users are diaspora** (Ukraine emigrants in US, Germany, etc.)
- Most popular genres: fiction, thrillers, literary prose, essays on military experience
- 9 of top 10 audiobooks were by **Ukrainian authors** — strong national preference

## User Research (CusDev)

Pain points confirmed through conversations with potential users:

1. **"I finish audiobooks but remember almost nothing"** — the #1 complaint
2. **"I can't pause and Google something without losing my place"** — AI chat solves this
3. **"I want to know what page I'm on in the real book"** — real page tracker feature
4. **"I have no idea what my friends are reading"** — social layer
5. **"I hear a word I don't know and just... skip it"** — clip + notes feature
